Every visit

Remember every visit.

Keep the whole story of a meal in one private place — long after the plates are cleared.

  • Rate food, service, ambience and value
  • Note noise level, dining type and number of diners
  • Save photos, receipts and personal observations
  • Mark business meals and generate Business Meal Reports
  • Get reminders about favorite dishes when you return
Every dish

Every dish, remembered.

ForkSmart keeps a history of the plates themselves — so you always know what to order again.

  • Save dish names, prices, photos and descriptions
  • Rate individual dishes for yourself and your loved ones
  • Add notes on what to order again — or how you’d change it
  • Review a dish’s order history across multiple visits
Your history

Explore your dining history.

Search, sort and rediscover every place you’ve been — and find your next meal on a map made from your own visits.

  • Browse restaurants or individual visits
  • Sort by visit date or your rating
  • Search by restaurant, dish, location, cuisine or notes
  • See your restaurants and ratings on a personalized map
  • Discover other dining options nearby
  • Compare ratings, price levels and previous visits
Preserve & share

Preserve and share the moment.

Turn a memorable meal into a beautiful, shareable postcard featuring your photo, rating and notes — perfect for anniversary dinners, vacations and meals worth remembering.

Your journal stays private. You decide if and what you share — nothing is posted publicly on your behalf.
