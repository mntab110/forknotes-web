Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a dining experience?

ForkSmart gives you a few ways to log a meal:

  • Scan a paper receipt — use your camera to capture the receipt and ForkSmart reads the details for you.
  • Add a receipt from your photo library — pick an existing photo of a receipt.
  • Import a PDF receipt — use a PDF receipt, such as an emailed or digital receipt.
  • Enter it manually — add the restaurant, dishes, ratings, and notes yourself.

Whichever method you choose, you can review and adjust everything before saving.

Why did ForkSmart not read my receipt correctly?

Receipt reading works best with a clear, well-lit photo where the entire receipt is visible and in focus. Blurry images, low light, glare, or a cropped or folded receipt can all reduce accuracy.

You don't have to rely on the automatic result — the extracted details (restaurant, dishes, and amounts) are always shown for you to review and correct before you save the experience.

How do I restore a purchase?

If you've already subscribed but your access isn't showing, open the subscription area in ForkSmart's Settings and use the option to restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you originally used to subscribe.

If your subscription still doesn't appear after restoring, please contact support.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

ForkSmart subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account, so they're managed in Apple's subscription settings rather than inside the app. From there you can change or cancel your plan.

Manage your subscription here: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Is my dining history public?

No. ForkSmart is a private personal dining journal, not a public review feed. Your experiences, ratings, notes, photos, and receipts are private by default and are not published for others to browse.

You may choose to share an individual experience to social media, but that's always your decision.

How do I export my information?

You can export your data at any time from Settings → Export Data in the app. ForkSmart lets you export your restaurants, experiences, and dishes, as well as your saved receipts and photos, so you can keep your own copy.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account directly in the app under Settings → Account → Delete Account. Before you confirm, we recommend exporting your data first so you keep a copy.

Deleting your account permanently removes your dining history, photos, and saved data. You'll be asked to confirm before the deletion is scheduled.

How does ForkSmart Partnership work?

Partnership lets two ForkSmart members link their accounts and share one combined dining history that both people can see and add to. It's designed for partners who dine out together and want a single shared journal.

Even with a shared history, each person keeps their own personal dish ratings and notes, so you can each record how you felt about a dish.

What happens when a Partnership ends?

When a partnership ends, ForkSmart separates the accounts and gives each person their own independent copy of the shared dining history, so you can both continue on your own. During the separation there's a short review window when shared editing is paused while the accounts are split.

Your personal dish ratings and notes stay with your account.

Which features require Plus or Pro?

ForkSmart offers free features along with additional capabilities on its paid plans. The most up-to-date list of what's included with Plus and Pro is shown on the subscription screen in the app, under Settings → Subscriptions, where you can also compare and choose a plan.
